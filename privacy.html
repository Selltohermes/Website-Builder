Who we are

Sell To Hermes ("we", "us") is a real estate investment company based in Palm Beach, Florida. You can reach us at (561) 336-5540 or Hermes@Selltohermes.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, phone number, email address, property address, property type, condition and selling timeline when you submit our offer form or call us.

How we use it

  • To prepare and deliver your cash offer.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties. We share it only with the service providers who help us operate, such as our form and email provider, our title company and our attorney, and only as needed to do their work.
